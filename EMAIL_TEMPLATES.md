## 🎯 Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
✅ Keep under 50 characters
✅ Be specific and clear
✅ Use personalization when possible
✅ Create urgency (when appropriate)
✅ Avoid spam trigger words

❌ Don't use ALL CAPS
❌ Don't overuse emojis
❌ Don't be misleading
❌ Don't use excessive punctuation!!!

### Email Body
✅ Start with recipient's name
✅ Get to the point quickly
✅ Use short paragraphs (2-3 lines)
✅ Include clear call-to-action
✅ Make it scannable (bullets, bold)
✅ End with next steps

❌ Don't write long paragraphs
❌ Don't use jargon
❌ Don't be too salesy
❌ Don't forget to proofread

### Timing
- **Onboarding:** Immediately after signup
- **Trial Reminder:** 3 days before expiration
- **Check-in:** 7, 30, 90 days after signup
- **Feature Announcement:** Mid-week, 10 AM
- **Re-engagement:** After 30 days of inactivity

---

**Use these templates as starting points. Personalize them for your specific situation and maintain your brand voice throughout!**
