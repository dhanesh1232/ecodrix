ECODrIx: Indian software company. Builds ERIX, one workspace for businesses that sell over WhatsApp, Instagram, email and their website. ERIX started August 2025, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. Founder: Dhanesh Mekalthuru (Dhanesh M, Erix). Early access via waitlist; pilots set up by the team.

ERIX 1.0 (live): ERIX CRM (contacts, pipeline, forms, appointments, proposals, projects with e-sign and client portal, GST invoices with Razorpay payment links), ERIX Connect (official WhatsApp API on your own account with no markup, Instagram DMs, email, website chat, one inbox), ERIX Flow (automations, approvals, "Wait for a reply", AI replies), AI agents (sales, channels, website), ERIX Storage (files).

Not live yet: ERIX LAIE (invite-only beta), voice agent (in development). Pricing not published during early access; billed in rupees with GST.
